Meet Our Founder

Mufti Hassan (Mauzu Hassan)

Founder & Chief Instructor, Dar Al-Ulum Wal Hikam Arabic & Islamic Institute Online

Knowledge is a trust from Allah. Our mission is to make authentic Arabic and Islamic education accessible to every student, wherever they may be, while nurturing faith, character, and excellence.

About

Mufti Hassan (Mauzu Hassan) is a dedicated educator and lifelong student of Arabic and Islamic sciences. His journey began with a deep love for the Qur'an and the Arabic language, which inspired him to pursue formal studies and years of teaching experience. Throughout his academic and professional development, he has remained committed to helping students understand Islam through authentic knowledge, sound methodology, and exemplary character.

In response to the growing need for accessible online Islamic education, he founded Dar Al-Ulum Wal Hikam Arabic & Islamic Institute Online. The institute was established with the vision of providing structured, affordable, and high-quality instruction in Arabic, Qur'anic studies, Tajweed, and Islamic sciences for learners of all ages and backgrounds. His goal is to inspire students not only to seek knowledge, but also to apply it in their lives and share it with others.

Vision

Our vision is to establish Dar Al-Ulum Wal Hikam as a globally recognized center of excellence for Arabic language and authentic Islamic education. We aspire to nurture knowledgeable, confident, and morally upright Muslims who will serve their communities with wisdom, sincerity, and compassion. Through quality teaching and accessible online learning, we aim to empower students worldwide to connect deeply with the Qur'an, the Sunnah, and the Arabic language.
